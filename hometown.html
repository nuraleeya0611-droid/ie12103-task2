My Hometown: Ranau, Sabah

Nestled in the highlands of Sabah, Ranau is known for its cool mountain air, lush valleys, and iconic natural landmarks at the foothills of Mount Kinabalu.

Mount Kinabalu

Towering over the Kundasang-Ranau highlands, offering breathtaking morning views and crisp highland weather.

Kundasang Highlands

Famous for rolling green pastures, fresh dairy produce, and vibrant highland vegetable markets.

Eco-Tourism

Located in Nalapak, Ranau, this lush organic tea plantation offers sweeping views of rolling green tea fields set against the majestic backdrop of Mount Kinabalu.
